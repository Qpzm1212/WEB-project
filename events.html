Weekend Entertainment

Join us at Meat Point Astana for an unforgettable evening of live entertainment and exceptional dining. Every weekend, our restaurant transforms into a vibrant venue featuring the best local musicians and special guest performances. Whether you are looking to unwind after a long week with smooth jazz or celebrate a special occasion with upbeat traditional Turkish tunes, our events are designed to create lasting memories.

Our live music nights run every Friday and Saturday from 8:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Because these evenings are incredibly popular among our guests, tables fill up quickly. We highly recommend registering in advance to secure your spot. Enjoy our signature halal steaks and kebabs while listening to fantastic live music. Please note that special conditions may apply for large groups, and a small deposit might be required to hold your reservation on busy nights. Fill out the registration form below to book your attendance for our upcoming weekend events. We look forward to welcoming you!

Important Note

The live music schedule is subject to change during national holidays. Please confirm your registration with our manager.
