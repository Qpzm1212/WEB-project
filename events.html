Weekend Entertainment

Join us at Meat Point Astana for an unforgettable evening of live entertainment and exceptional dining. Every weekend, our restaurant transforms into a vibrant venue featuring the best local musicians. We offer a variety of performances:

  • Friday Nights
    • Smooth Jazz
    • Acoustic Pop
  • Saturday Nights
    • Traditional Turkish Tunes

Because these evenings are incredibly popular among our guests, tables fill up quickly. We highly recommend registering in advance. Listen to what our guests have to say:

"The live music on Saturday was absolutely fantastic! It perfectly complemented the delicious steaks and created a magical atmosphere for our anniversary dinner." — Aibek S.
