Weekend Entertainment

Join us at Meat Point Astana for an unforgettable evening of live entertainment and exceptional dining. Every weekend, our restaurant transforms into a vibrant venue featuring the best local musicians and special guest performances. Whether you are looking to unwind after a long week with smooth jazz or celebrate a special occasion with upbeat traditional Turkish tunes, our events are designed to create lasting memories. Follow our resident musicians on their Instagram page.

The Friday jazz set turned an ordinary dinner into the best night out we've had in Astana this year.

This Week's Program

  • Friday — Smooth Jazz Night:
    • 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM: Warm-up set
    • 9:30 PM – 11:00 PM: Main performance
  • Saturday — Turkish Tunes Night:
    • 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM: Acoustic guitar
    • 9:30 PM – 11:00 PM: Full band with vocals

Our live music nights run every Friday and Saturday, and tables fill up quickly, so we recommend registering in advance. Enjoy our signature halal steaks and kebabs while you listen. Please note that special conditions may apply for large groups, and a deposit might be required on busy nights.

How Registration Works

  1. Manager confirms your seat by phone.
  2. We check availability for the chosen date.
  3. You submit the registration form below.

Important Note

The live music schedule is subject to change during national holidays. Please confirm your registration with our manager.
