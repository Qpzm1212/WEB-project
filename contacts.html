Get in Touch with Us

Getting in touch with Meat Point Astana is incredibly easy and convenient for all our beloved guests. Whether you want to ask a question about our menu, clarify the availability of our summer terrace, or simply leave your valuable feedback, we are always here to help. You can use the form below to submit a reservation request or send us a direct message. Our administration team checks the inbox regularly and strives to reply to every inquiry as quickly as possible.

If you are planning to visit us in person, finding our building is very straightforward. We are located in the modern EXPO district. Please refer to the table below for our exact working hours, although we proudly operate around the clock. Your comfort is our top priority! Please note that during the weekends, the tables are booked very fast, so it is highly recommended to reserve your spot in advance.

Note: Special conditions apply for booking large corporate banquets and birthdays.
