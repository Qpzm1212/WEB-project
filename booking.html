Welcome to Meat Point

Welcome to the official booking page of the halal restaurant Meat Point in Astana. We are proud to offer our guests authentic Turkish cuisine prepared according to traditional family recipes. Whether you are planning a fast business lunch, a romantic evening, or a large family celebration, we have the perfect table for you. Our cozy interior and attentive staff will ensure your maximum comfort. As our chef always says, Hospitality is the soul of every meal.

Please carefully read our booking rules below. We strongly recommend booking tables for the weekend at least 24 hours in advance, as we host live acoustic music and the number of available seats is strictly limited. All our drinks are served with pure H2O filtered water for premium quality.

Booking Rules & Guidelines

  1. Confirmation of the reservation by a manager via direct phone call.
  2. System processing of your table preferences and guest details.
  3. Filling out and submitting the online application form below.
  • Conditions for large groups and banquets:
    • Advance deposit requirement for groups of 10 or more people.
    • Dish pre-order must be placed at least 24 hours in advance.
