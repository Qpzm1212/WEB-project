Guest Policy

Reservation Guidelines & Policies

To ensure a seamless Turkish culinary experience, please review our table reservation policy before booking.

  • ✔️ Instant Call Confirmation: Our hostess contacts you within 15 minutes to verify party size and special seating requests.
  • ✔️ 15-Minute Grace Period: Reserved tables are held for 15 minutes past the booking time before release to walk-in guests.
  • ✔️ Group Deposit (10+ Guests): Parties of 10 or more require an advance deposit of 3,500 ₸ per guest to guarantee banquet seating.
  • ✔️ VIP Room Amenities: Private hall features dedicated service, acoustic music controls, and personalized banquet menus.
  • ✔️ 100% Certified Halal: Premium local beef and lamb prepared fresh daily on open wood-fired charcoal grills.
  • ✔️ Special Occasions: Celebrating a birthday or anniversary? Mention it in advance for complimentary tea ceremony and dessert.
