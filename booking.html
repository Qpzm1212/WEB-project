Welcome to Meat Point

Welcome to the booking page of the halal restaurant Meat Point in Astana. We are proud to offer our guests authentic Turkish cuisine prepared according to traditional recipes. Whether you are planning a business lunch, a romantic dinner, or a large family celebration, we have the perfect table for you. Our cozy interior and attentive staff will ensure your maximum comfort.

Please carefully read our booking rules. We strongly recommend booking tables for the weekend at least a day in advance, as we have live music and the number of available seats is strictly limited.

Booking Rules

  1. Confirmation of the reservation by a manager via phone.
  2. Data processing.
  3. Filling out the form below.
  • Conditions for large groups:
    • Deposit for groups of 10 or more people.
    • Pre-order at least 24 hours in advance.
