Authentic Turkish Cuisine

Welcome to the culinary heart of Meat Point Astana. Our menu brings you the authentic taste of Turkish cuisine, made from fresh, strictly halal ingredients sourced from trusted local farmers.

From hearty main courses with our signature premium beef and lamb to light salads and traditional Turkish beverages, there is something for everyone. Our meat is grilled over an open fire for that distinct smoky flavor. Ask our staff about the chef's daily specials.
