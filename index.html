Welcome to Meat Point Astana

Meat Point is a cozy Turkish restaurant in Astana, located at 45 Uly Dala Avenue, not far from the Botanical Garden and the EXPO district. The establishment specializes in Turkish cuisine and places particular emphasis on grilled meat dishes. The menu features such popular items as Adana kebab, Meat Point’s signature dish with an assortment of meat and side dishes, and a juicy beef steak. Guests can also enjoy chicken, fish, pide, pizza, salads, and traditional Turkish drinks.

Meat Point is suitable both for an everyday lunch and for meetings with friends, family dinners, and special events. Thanks to its 24‑hour operating hours, the restaurant is convenient to visit at any time of day — you can start the day with breakfast, drop by for a business lunch, or have dinner in the late evening. The restaurant offers delivery and takeout options for guests, and its cozy atmosphere and summer terrace allow you to choose the right format for your leisure time. The restaurant also has facilities for family celebrations, birthdays, and corporate events.

A special place in the Meat Point concept is occupied by the variety of meat dishes and traditional flavors of Turkish cuisine. On weekdays from 12:00 to 15:00, guests can take advantage of the business lunch offer, which includes soup, a main course, a salad, tea, and bread. Among the dishes that deserve special attention is the signature Meat Point assortment, which combines izgar kofte, Adana kebab, lamb and chicken shashlik, mini pide, mini lahmacun, and side dishes. On Fridays and Saturdays, the restaurant’s atmosphere is complemented by live music evenings.

Meat Point strives to combine the rich flavors of Turkish cuisine, high‑quality products, and a welcoming atmosphere in which it’s pleasant to spend time with loved ones or friends. Here, every guest can choose a dish to suit their taste — from aromatic kebab and grilled meat to fresh salads, pide, and desserts. The 24‑hour format, diverse menu, and the option to order dishes with delivery make the restaurant a convenient place at any time of day. Drop by Meat Point at 45 Uly Dala Avenue and discover the taste of authentic Turkish cuisine in Astana.
