Welcome to Meat Point Astana Top Rated

Meat Point is a cozy Turkish restaurant in Astana, located at 45 Uly Dala Avenue, near the Botanical Garden and the EXPO district. The establishment specializes in authentic Turkish cuisine with particular emphasis on grilled meat dishes. As culinary guide Taste of East noted, Traditional roasting requires both patience and passion.

Our restaurant is suitable for everyday lunch, meetings with friends, family dinners, and special celebrations. Thanks to our 24/7 operating hours, you can visit us at any time — start your day with a traditional breakfast or drop by for a late dinner. All non-alcoholic cold beverages are served with purified H2O iced water.

On weekdays from 12:00 to 15:00, guests can take advantage of our business lunch offer. Among our special dishes, the signature Meat Point assortment deserves special attention. On Fridays and Saturdays, the dining atmosphere is complemented by live acoustic music. Table pre-booking is recommended on weekends.

Note: Delivery and takeout options are available 24 hours a day across Astana.
