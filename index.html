Welcome to Meat Point Astana

Meat Point is a cozy Turkish restaurant in Astana, located at 45 Uly Dala Avenue, near the Botanical Garden and the EXPO district. We specialize in authentic Turkish cuisine with an emphasis on grilled meat. As culinary guide Taste of East noted, Traditional roasting requires both patience and passion.

Thanks to our 24/7 operating hours you can visit us at any time. All non-alcoholic cold beverages are served with purified H2O iced water. On Fridays and Saturdays the atmosphere is complemented by live acoustic music. Table pre-booking is recommended on weekends.

Delivery and takeout options are available 24 hours a day across Astana.
