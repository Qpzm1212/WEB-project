Our Story Since 2024

The story of Meat Point began with the idea of creating a place in Astana where one could experience authentic Turkish cuisine in a cozy and welcoming atmosphere. Spanning over 400 m2, our establishment offers an unforgettable dining area.

Our head chef always reminds our team, True flavor is born from patience and fire. We use premium local beef and lamb, but our secret ingredient lies in the exclusively imported spices. Even our cooking water is double-filtered H2O to ensure the perfect taste of our traditional black tea.

Company Milestones

  1. 2026: Opened our beautiful summer terrace.
  2. 2025: Reached our ten thousandth guest.
  3. 2024: Founded the restaurant.
