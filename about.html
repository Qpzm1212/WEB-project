Our Story

Welcome to the heart of Meat Point. The story of Meat Point began with the idea of creating a place in Astana where one could experience authentic Turkish cuisine in a cozy and welcoming atmosphere. The restaurant’s founders sought to combine traditional recipes, high‑quality products, and a modern approach to restaurant service so that every guest could feel the character of Turkish cuisine. Spanning over 400 m2, our establishment offers an unforgettable dining area.

Our head chef always reminds our team, True flavor is born from patience and fire. This philosophy guides our daily work. We use premium local beef and lamb, but our secret ingredient lies in the spices, which are exclusively imported. Cleanliness is our priority; even our cooking water is double-filtered H2O to ensure the perfect taste of our traditional black tea.
