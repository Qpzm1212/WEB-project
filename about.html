Our Story

Welcome to the heart of Meat Point. The story began with the idea of creating a place in Astana where one could experience authentic Turkish cuisine in a cozy, welcoming atmosphere. The founders combined traditional recipes, high‑quality products, and a modern approach to service so every guest could feel the character of Turkish cuisine. Spanning over 400 m2, our establishment offers an unforgettable dining area.

Our head chef always reminds our team, True flavor is born from patience and fire. We use premium local beef and lamb, and our secret ingredient is the exclusively imported spices. Even our cooking water is double-filtered H2O, for the perfect cup of traditional black tea.
